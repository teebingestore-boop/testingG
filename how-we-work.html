FROM FIRST DRAWING TO FINAL DETAIL COORDINATED

From First Drawing to Final Detail.

Every step is focused on one goal: accurate detailing that helps your steel project move smoothly from design to fabrication and erection.

GSD

Review. Model. Coordinate. Detail. Deliver. Review. Support.

01
📐
STEP 01

Review & Project Planning

We start by studying the available architectural and structural drawings, project requirements, specifications, and schedules. This helps us understand the scope and establish a clear detailing approach from the beginning.

↓
02
🏗️
STEP 02

Structural 3D Modeling

Once the project requirements are clear, we develop a detailed 3D structural steel model using Tekla Structures. The model provides the foundation for detailing, shop drawings, connections, and material information.

↓
03
🔩
STEP 03

Connections & Model Coordination

We develop and detail the required connections, including bolts, welds, plates, clips, and other connection components. Our models are also coordinated with the structural design to identify and resolve potential issues early.

↓
04
📋
STEP 04

Shop Drawings & Fabrication Detailing

From the completed model, we produce clear and detailed shop drawings containing the views, dimensions, sections, marks, and information needed by fabricators for accurate steel production.

↓
05
⚙️
STEP 05

Material Lists & CNC Files

We prepare detailed material information and generate the required fabrication data, including BOMs and CNC-ready files such as DSTV and PFXT , helping simplify material planning and fabrication.

↓
06
🔍
STEP 06

Review, Updates & Finalization

The drawings and model are shared for review, and we incorporate required comments or revisions. We continue refining the deliverables until the project requirements are properly addressed.

↓
07
🏭
STEP 07

Fabrication & Site Support

Our involvement can continue beyond drawing delivery. We provide detailing support during fabrication and erection, helping address field queries, drawing updates, and coordination requirements when needed.
