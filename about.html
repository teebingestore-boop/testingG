WHO WE ARE

Engineering ideas into buildable reality.

At Grid Steel Detailing, we believe that great steel structures begin with accurate information, thoughtful coordination, and attention to every detail.

We are a structural steel detailing and BIM-focused company dedicated to transforming engineering and architectural designs into clear, accurate, and fabrication-ready documentation.

Our work sits at the intersection of engineering, technology, and construction. From structural steel shop drawings and erection drawings to 3D BIM modeling, connection detailing, and coordination, we help contractors, fabricators, engineers, and construction teams move from design to fabrication and installation with greater confidence.

With a detail-driven approach and a strong understanding of construction requirements, we focus on producing drawings and models that are technically accurate, practical, organized, and easy to understand on the fabrication floor and at the job site.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Precision creates confidence.

We don't just produce drawings. We create information that helps project teams fabricate, coordinate and erect steel with confidence.

OUR APPROACH

Built Around Accuracy.

Structural steel is a system where every component matters. A small discrepancy in a dimension, connection, member location, or drawing can create challenges during fabrication or erection.

That is why accuracy is at the core of everything we do.

Every project is approached with careful drawing review, model coordination, detailed checking, and a commitment to maintaining consistency throughout the documentation process.

Our goal is simple: make the detailing process more precise, predictable, and efficient.

TECHNOLOGY + EXPERTISE

Technology meets construction expertise.

Modern construction demands more than traditional 2D drafting. With BIM and advanced 3D modeling workflows, structural information can be developed, reviewed, coordinated, and communicated with significantly greater clarity.

Grid Detailing utilizes modern detailing and modeling workflows to create intelligent 3D structural models and comprehensive project documentation.

Technology is important — but we believe technology is most valuable when it is combined with practical construction understanding.
